Welcome to My Snippets

Right-click any text input, textarea, or editable field and insert a predefined value from a nested menu. We've pre-loaded a few demo folders so you can try it right away — read on to make it yours.

1

Try the demo values

Right-click into any text field on a page and look for the My Snippets submenu. Pick a value to see it fill the field.

2

Replace them with your own

Open the Options page to add, edit, delete, and reorder your own folders and values. Delete the demo entries whenever you're ready — they're only there to show how folders can nest.

3

Finding the Options page later

This welcome tab only opens once, right after install. To get back to your snippets afterward, go to your browser's extensions page (chrome://extensions on Chrome/Brave, edge://extensions on Edge), find My Snippets, and click Details → Extension options. If you've pinned the extension's icon to your toolbar, right-clicking it also offers an Options shortcut.

4

Need help later?

Right-click any text field, open the My Snippets submenu, and pick Help below the divider at the bottom. It covers finding the Options page, frequently asked questions, and how to report a bug.
