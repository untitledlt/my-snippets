My Snippets — Help

Answers to common questions, plus how to find your settings or report a problem.

How to find the Options page

The Options page is where you add, edit, delete, and reorder your snippet folders and values. There are three ways to open it:

  • Right-click any text field, open the My Snippets submenu, and pick Options below the divider at the bottom.
  • Go to your browser's extensions page (chrome://extensions on Chrome/Brave, edge://extensions on Edge), find My Snippets, and click Details → Extension options.
  • If you've pinned the extension's icon to your toolbar, right-click it and choose Options.

Frequently asked questions

Why don't I see the My Snippets menu when I right-click?

The menu only appears on editable fields — text inputs, textareas, and contenteditable elements. Right-clicking elsewhere on the page (like plain text or an image) won't show it. If it's still missing on a field, try reloading the page so the extension's content script can attach.

Can I organize snippets into folders?

Yes. The Options page lets you nest folders as deep as you like, and each folder becomes a submenu in the right-click menu.

Is my snippet data synced or sent anywhere?

Snippets are always stored locally in your browser. If your browser's own sync is turned on (signed into Chrome/Edge, or a Brave Sync chain), they're also mirrored through that sync so they follow you to your other devices. The extension itself never sends anything to an external server — the only "network" involved is your browser's own sync, which you control from its settings, not from this extension. See the privacy policy for details.

How do I remove the demo folders?

Open the Options page and delete the demo entries like any other folder or value — they're only there to show how nesting works and aren't required.

Why did the order of items in my submenu change unexpectedly?

The right-click menu rebuilds itself whenever your snippets change (edits save automatically on the Options page), so the order always reflects the current order of folders and values there. Reorder them on the Options page to change how they appear.
