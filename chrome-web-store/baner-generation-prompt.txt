Background context (for tone only, not to be rendered as text):
My Snippets lets you right-click any <input>, <textarea>, or contenteditable
field and insert a saved snippet from a nested context menu — no autofill,
no field matching, just a manual "paste this value here" tool. Snippets are
organized into folders of any depth. They're stored locally on your device,
and optionally mirrored through your browser's own built-in sync (Chrome,
Edge, Brave, etc.) if you already have that enabled — no extra account, no
server of ours involved. The extension is free and open source.
