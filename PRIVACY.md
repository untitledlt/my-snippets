# Privacy Policy — My Snippets

My Snippets does not collect, transmit, or share any data with us or any
third party.

- Your snippets are stored on your own machine, scoped to your browser
  profile, using the browser's standard extension storage.
- If your browser's built-in sync is turned on (e.g. signed into Chrome or
  Edge, or a Brave Sync chain), your snippets are also mirrored through
  that sync feature — the same mechanism your browser already uses to sync
  bookmarks and passwords. That sync is encrypted in transit and at rest by
  your browser vendor, not by us; we have no server and never see this
  data. If sync is off, or exceeds your browser's sync size limit, your
  data simply stays on the device.
- The extension itself makes no network requests of any kind. The only
  data transmission that can happen is your browser's own sync feature,
  which you control from your browser's own settings — this extension
  cannot turn it on or off.
- No analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting is included.
- The content script only reads the field you right-click in order to
  insert the value you selected; it does not read, log, or transmit page
  content, form data, or browsing activity.
- The Options page lets you export your snippets to a JSON file, or
  restore from one. This is a local file saved to or read from your own
  disk through your browser's normal file dialogs — it is never uploaded
  anywhere.
- Uninstalling the extension removes all locally stored data with it (data
  already synced by your browser is governed by your browser's own sync
  settings).

If you have questions about this policy, open an issue on the project's
repository: https://github.com/untitledlt/my-snippets
