# Privacy Policy — Chrome Filler ("My Snippets")

Chrome Filler does not collect, transmit, or share any data.

- All values you configure are stored only in `chrome.storage.local`, on
  your own machine, scoped to your browser profile.
- The extension makes no network requests of any kind.
- No analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting is included.
- The content script only reads the field you right-click in order to
  insert the value you selected; it does not read, log, or transmit page
  content, form data, or browsing activity.
- Uninstalling the extension removes all stored data with it.

If you have questions about this policy, open an issue on the project's
repository.
